# Loreforge UX review, 2026-10-29

A look-and-feel and usability pass over 0.2.0-beta, judged as if Loreforge were
a web app people choose to open for fun. Read from source only: `Host.lua`,
`Setup.lua`, `Options.lua`, `Player.lua`, `Core.lua` and the chat copy in
`Game.lua`. No client was run, so anything about pixel fit still needs a
screenshot.

## Overall

The engine and recovery logic are solid, and the player window is close to
good. The host side is where the product falls down. It reads as an operator
console: pixel-pinned widgets, pipe-joined status strings, a row of five
footer buttons and a long settings form before anything fun happens. The host
is the person who decides whether Loreforge gets used, so their first two
minutes matter most, and right now those minutes are a form.

The second gap is feel. There's no sound, no colour change as time runs out,
no celebration for a win, no keyboard answers and a flat records dump. Those
are the things that make people want to open it again.

## Priority 1: fix before the next beta

1. **Two identical "Open Party lobby" buttons.** With Party available and no
   lobby, `setup.start` reads "Open Party lobby" (`Setup.lua:58`) and
   `setup.lobby` also reads "Open Party lobby" (`Setup.lua:60`). Both do the
   same thing. Keep one primary action. Suggested flow: primary is
   **Open lobby** before a lobby exists, then **Start game** once it does;
   **Invite new arrivals** and **Close lobby** become secondary actions shown
   only while the lobby is open.

2. **The lobby is a count, not a screen.** The host's only sign of who joined
   is "| 2 joined in addon" appended to the muted pool line
   (`Setup.lua:63`). This is the moment players are gathering and the host
   wants to see names. Give the lobby its own view: each party member with a
   state (Joined, Invited, Chat only, Declined), the game summary and a big
   Start button.

3. **Setup is a form, with no quick start.** Twelve controls across five
   sections plus ten categories, with jump buttons (`Setup.lua:105`) because
   it doesn't fit. Add presets at the top (for example Quick: 1 x 5, Standard:
   3 x 5, Marathon: 5 x 10) that set rounds, questions and timer, and fold
   Pacing, Ready check and Results into a collapsed **Advanced** section. Most
   hosts should only touch Output, length and categories.

4. **No estimated game length.** The pool line says "861 questions | 15
   needed | 4 eligible players". Add "about 9 minutes" from questions x
   (answer time + reveal + gap). This is the number a host actually asks
   their group about.

5. **Errors point elsewhere.** The top status says "Game setup needs
   attention. See the message below." (`Setup.lua:49`) and the real message
   sits in muted text at the bottom of a 590px window. Put the problem next
   to the control that fixes it (Output dropdown, categories) and put it on
   the disabled Start button's tooltip.

6. **Host Escape does nothing.** The player window registers in
   `UISpecialFrames` (`Player.lua:220`); the host table doesn't. It's secure,
   so it can't close in combat, but out of combat Escape should close it like
   every other window. A small out-of-combat key handler works.

7. **"Not now" means never.** On an invitation, **Not now** declines and
   dismisses that session for good (`Player.lua:820`). The label promises the
   opposite. Either rename it **Decline**, or keep a "Join quiz" restore
   button available until the game starts.

## Priority 2: the live host table

8. **One countdown, shown properly.** During answers the same seconds appear
   in the status line, the primary button ("Answers open: 14s") and nowhere
   visual. The player window already has a timer bar; give the host the same
   bar and a large number, and remove the seconds from the status string.

9. **Replace the pipe status line.** "Answers open | Party | Host-paced |
   14s | 3 replies" (`Host.lua:375`) is five facts at the same weight. Split
   it: phase as a heading, Round 2/3 and Question 4/5 as progress pips, mode
   and channel as small tags, and replies as "3 of 5 answered" next to the
   leaderboard.

10. **Developer words in the UI.** "Waiting for chat echo",
    "Confirming chat...", "Waiting for addon question delivery",
    "click-to-send" and "Resend line" all describe internals. Suggested
    replacements: "Checking your message posted", "Posting to Party...",
    "Sending the question to addon players", "Manual posting", "Post again".

11. **Recovery should be one banner.** When a line stalls, the host gets a
    paused status, an instruction line, a preview line and up to three
    buttons spread around the window ("I saw this line in chat: continue",
    Resume, Resend line). Show one alert banner above the question: the line
    text, "Did this appear in chat?", and **Yes, continue** / **Post again**.

12. **Footer button soup.** Primary, mode toggle, Pause, Resend and Stop sit
    in one row. The mode button's label is the action, not the state
    ("Use host pacing" while auto, "Auto-advance" while host-paced,
    "Retry auto send" in fallback), which reads backwards. Make pacing a
    two-state segmented toggle showing the current mode, keep Pause and Stop
    as icon or small buttons on the right, and leave the primary action alone
    on the left.

13. **Leaderboard is plain text.** "Name  3 pts | streak 2", ten per page,
    with **More scores** cycling pages with no page indicator (`Host.lua:282`).
    Add rank numbers, highlight the leader, show who answered this question
    (tick or dot), and show "Page 2 of 3" or just scroll. Answered/unanswered
    per player during the timer is the single most useful thing a host can
    see.

14. **The host never sees the player card.** The host table draws questions
    as plain labels and its own answers as "Locked: 2) ..." buttons. Reuse the
    player window's question card (numbered rows, reveal colours, timer) for
    the host view. One visual language, less code, and the host sees what
    players see.

## Priority 3: feel and delight

15. **Sound.** Nothing calls `PlaySound` anywhere. A soft cue for
    invitation, question open, last five seconds, correct and wrong, and a
    fanfare for the winner would do more for "want to use it" than any layout
    change. Make it a setting, on by default, with a volume-respecting
    channel.

16. **Urgency on the timer.** The bar is always the `selected` blue
    (`Player.lua:161`). Shift to amber under 30% and red under five seconds,
    and pulse the restore button when it reads "Loreforge: answer now".

17. **Reveal is a 1px border.** Correct and wrong answers only change
    `T.Border` (`Player.lua:383`). Fill the correct row with a green tint and
    the wrong pick with a red tint, and show a short "+1" next to the
    player's score.

18. **Finish needs a moment.** Final results open with "Final results from
    X." and a standings list. Show a headline for the local player ("You won",
    "2nd of 5", "Shared win"), a top-three podium, and their best streak and
    accuracy. That's the screenshot people post in guild chat.

19. **Keyboard answers.** Add keybindings for Answer 1 to 4 via
    `Bindings.xml` (they're ordinary button clicks, not protected actions),
    and show the key on each row. Mid-dungeon chat typing is the slowest way
    to answer.

20. **Records are a text dump.** `ShowRecords` builds lines sorted by
    character key (`Host.lua:225`) and shows them in a copy box. Make it a
    Hall of Fame: sorted by wins, then accuracy, with columns, the local
    player highlighted and the copy button kept as a secondary action. Same
    idea for the player's **Saved games** list (date, host, place,
    score/total is good, give it a placement colour).

21. **Category picker.** Ten checkboxes, each with the same tooltip
    ("Combine categories...", `Setup.lua:116`). Show the available question
    count per category for the current client and difficulty, add All and
    None, and lean into the fun names (Murlocs and Mischief, Things You
    Probably Vendored) with an icon each. Remove the repeated "Applies to the
    next game." slider hint too (`Setup.lua:84`); it's on every slider and
    tells the host nothing.

22. **A practice entry point.** Solo Party output already runs a local test,
    but the host only finds that by accident. A visible **Practice solo**
    action on first open is the easiest way to show someone what Loreforge
    is in thirty seconds.

23. **Brand.** The title is "Loreforge | Host game" in accent text. The
    About page already has a tavern voice ("AT THE TAVERN", the tips). Put the
    book icon in the title bar, use the `flavour` parchment colour for
    question text and headings, and keep green/blue for state only. Right now
    green accent and blue selected compete on the same screen.

24. **Player window scale.** `ns.db.playerScale` is read but there's no
    control (noted in `player-ui.md`). Add a slider to the Party player
    settings page.

## Chat copy

The chat side is the experience for players without the addon, so it counts
as UI. It's mostly good and terse. Two notes:

- The `[LF G12]` prefix (`Game.lua:85`) looks like debug output to people
  who don't know the addon. If echo matching needs a unique tag, something
  like `[Loreforge #12]` reads as intentional.
- The opening line "3 rounds x 5 questions. Starts in 10s. Type ready;
  answer 1-4." could name the categories in a few words, so the group knows
  what they're in for.

## Second pass

Covers what the first pass skipped: Party lobby and history behaviour in
`Party.lua`, the HammerCore settings shell and widgets, chat volume, session
edge cases and wording consistency.

### Structure

25. **Five surfaces, no home.** Loreforge currently spreads across the host
    table, the shared settings window (Player records, Party player,
    Visibility, Theme and the reference pages), a copy box for records, the
    player window and the minimap. `/lf` opens either the host table or the
    player window depending on hidden state (`Core.lua:34`). One main window
    with tabs (Play, History, Records) and a Settings link would give people
    a place to come back to. Play shows setup, lobby, live game or joined
    match depending on state.

26. **History and records are split by accident.** Party matches go to
    **Saved games** (player window, reached from Settings > Party player or a
    finished screen). Raid, Instance and Guild games only update records,
    which live in a copy box under Settings > Player records. A host has no
    path to either from the host table. Put both in the History tab, with
    chat-only games listed too (date, channel, winner, your place).

27. **Settings opens on top of the host table.** Both windows are `DIALOG`
    strata and nearly the same size (`Window.lua:167`, `Host.lua:239`), so
    **Settings** buries the game behind it. Once there's a main window this
    goes away. Until then, offset the settings window or hide the host table
    while settings is open.

### Lobby and session

28. **Changing Output kills the lobby silently.** With a lobby open,
    switching the Output dropdown away from Party cancels it and tells
    joined players "The host changed the match channel" (`Party.lua:518`).
    The host gets no warning. Lock game settings while a lobby is open, or
    confirm first.

29. **The lobby expires after 10 minutes without warning.** `h.expires` is
    600 seconds (`Party.lua:259`). Joined players are told it expired; the
    host just finds the setup screen back. Show a lobby timer, warn at one
    minute and let the host extend it.

30. **A host reload ends the game with no trace.** Game state is in memory
    only. After `/reload` or a disconnect the host gets a fresh setup screen,
    players see "The host is unavailable", and nothing explains what
    happened. Resuming is a bigger job, but at minimum the host should see
    "Your last game was interrupted at question 7 of 15" with an option to
    post the standings so far.

31. **Leave has no confirmation.** It's a red button in the footer on every
    question (`Player.lua:202`), and leaving takes the player out of the
    match on the host's side. Ask once ("Leave this match? Your points so
    far stay on the board.").

32. **Invitations take over the screen.** An invite opens the full player
    window at the centre-top of the screen while someone might be questing.
    A compact toast (host name, game length, **Join** / **Decline**) that
    expands into the window after joining would be less intrusive and still
    hard to miss.

### Player window details

33. **Numbering differs between host and player.** The host shows
    "Round 1/3 | Q2/5" (per round, `Host.lua:462`); the player window shows
    "Round 1/3 · Question 7/15" (whole game, `Player.lua:312`). Pick one.
    I'd use per round in the label and progress pips for the whole game.

34. **Between questions gives no clue what's next.** Players see "Waiting for
    the next question." whether auto-advance will fire in five seconds or the
    host is chatting. Send the next-question time when auto-advance is on and
    show a countdown; otherwise say "The host will start the next question."

35. **Presenting state contradicts itself.** During `presenting` the question
    and greyed answers are already visible while the status reads "Next
    question coming up." Use something like "Get ready, answers open in a
    moment."

36. **Saved match detail says the same thing twice.** It shows "Final results
    from X." from `STATUS.finished` and then "Saved results from X" from the
    model status (`Party.lua:353`). Keep one, and add the date.

37. **Only 8 of 20 saved matches are visible.** History keeps 20
    (`Party.lua:4`) but the window draws 8 (`Player.lua:12`) with no paging or
    scroll for the rest.

38. **No window scale for the host.** The player window has a (hidden) scale
    setting; the host table is fixed at 760 x 590 with mostly small fonts.
    The host reads it for the whole game, so it needs the same scale option.

### Setup controls

39. **Sliders are too fine for their ranges.** HammerCore sliders are at most
    180 px (`Widgets.lua:272`). Questions per round 1 to 50 gives about
    3.6 px per step, answer time 5 to 120 about 1.5 px per step, with no
    mouse wheel or arrow buttons, and values show without units ("20", not
    "20s"). Add wheel and arrow steps, show units, and narrow the ranges to
    what's sensible (answer time in 5 s steps, up to 15 questions per round,
    10 rounds). Presets from item 3 cover most of this.

40. **Game Master participation is off by default.** In a small Party of
    friends the host usually wants to play too, and the host table shows
    each question no earlier than everyone else. Consider defaulting it on
    for Party and off for Guild, or asking once on first use.

### Chat output

41. **Five chat lines per question can be two.** Every question posts the
    question line plus four choice lines (`Game.lua:201` to `203`). I checked
    the whole bank: all 1,000 questions fit their four choices on one line
    with the tag (longest 174 of 255 characters, median 74). Question plus a
    choices line halves the time per question, cuts chat noise for non-addon
    players and shortens every automatic gap. Keep the five-line layout as a
    setting if anyone prefers it.

42. **End-of-game records flood big channels.** With **Announce closing
    player records** on (the default), every player gets a line after the
    final scores (`Game.lua:372`). In a 25-person guild game that's 25 lines
    at two-second gaps, close to a minute of spam after the game ends. Limit
    it to the top three, or the winner plus personal bests, and keep the
    full table in the addon.

43. **One-round games announce the winner twice.** The last question of a
    round posts round results, then the final results follow straight after
    (`Game.lua:337` to `341`). With one round both lines say the same thing.
    Skip round results when there's only one round, or on the final round.

### Wording

44. **Pick one word for each thing.** Across the UI strings "host" appears 32
    times and "Game Master" 7; "game" 40, "match" 20, "quiz" 8 and "trivia"
    6. Players see "Join quiz", "Saved games", "Saved matches",
    "Party trivia match" and "Final results" for the same concept. I'd use
    **host** and **game** everywhere, with "quiz" only in friendly copy.

## Suggested order

If this becomes a work round, I'd take it in four passes so each is
testable in the client on its own:

1. Setup and lobby: items 1 to 7, 21, 22, 28, 29, 39, 40. Smallest code,
   biggest first impression.
2. Chat output: items 41 to 43. Small engine changes, and they help every
   player whether or not they have the addon.
3. Live host table and player window: items 8 to 14, 31, 33 to 38, ideally
   by sharing the player card.
4. Structure and feel: items 15 to 20, 23 to 27, 30, 32, 44.

Apart from items 30 and 41 to 43, none of this changes the engine, transport
or record rules. It's presentation in `Setup.lua`, `Host.lua`, `Player.lua`,
`Options.lua` and `Party.lua`'s model fields, plus a `Bindings.xml` and sound
calls.
